The case for a booth

Where else do buyers come to you?

Cold calls get ignored. Ads get scrolled past. The Home Expo puts you in a two-day conversation with people who already want what you sell.

Pre-qualified foot traffic

Nobody spends a Saturday at a home show by accident. Attendees arrive with a project, a budget and a list — many of them with photos and measurements in hand.

Ahead of the busy season

Mid-March is exactly when homeowners start lining up spring and summer work — so the conversations you have on the floor feed the next quarter's schedule.

Borrowed credibility

Exhibiting alongside established local builders and suppliers, under a 30-year-old association banner, is a trust signal you can't buy with advertising.

Real conversations, not clicks

Two days of face-to-face selling with people who can ask you the awkward questions and get them answered on the spot. That's a much shorter path to winning the work.

Listed before the doors open

Every confirmed exhibitor appears in the online directory with a description and a link back to your site — working for you in the weeks leading up to the show.

Promotion we pay for

The GRBIA runs the advertising — radio, print, digital and social across the Green River region — so the crowd is our job, not yours.

Good to know

  • Booth placement is assigned by show management, based on comparable available space. Apply early for the best position.
  • To reserve space you'll need payment in full and proof of public liability insurance.
  • Cheques are payable to the Home Builders Association of Owensboro.
  • Exhibitor Reception: Friday, March 12, 6–8 p.m. at the HBAO Building — come meet the other exhibitors before the doors open.
  • Booths must stay staffed through both days' published show hours.
