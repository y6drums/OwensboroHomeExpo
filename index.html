Why bother coming

Compare a dozen contractors before lunch

The slow way to plan a home project is four phone calls, three no-shows and a month of waiting on quotes. The fast way is a Saturday morning at the Convention Center.

  • Meet local pros in person — no gatekeeping, no phone tag
  • See products in the flesh before you commit to them
  • Collect side-by-side estimates from competing vendors
  • Ask what a job really costs, without an in-home sales visit
  • Bring the kids — it's free, indoors and stroller-friendly
