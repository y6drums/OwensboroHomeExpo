Boilerplate

The Owensboro Home Expo is presented annually by the Green River Building Industry Association at the Owensboro Convention Center. Now in its fourth decade, the two-day show fills 44,000 square feet with builders, remodelers, landscapers, suppliers and home service providers from across the Green River region. Admission is free to the public. The GRBIA, founded in 1958, promotes, educates and advocates for homeownership through the residential and commercial construction industry.
