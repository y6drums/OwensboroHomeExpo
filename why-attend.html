Five hours here saves you five weeks of phone calls

Every business on this floor sells something for your home — and every one of them is standing right there, ready to answer questions, quote a job and show you the actual product.

Worth the Saturday

Why people keep coming back

01

Everyone's in one room

Sixty-plus businesses, one floor, two days. Comparing three roofers usually means three separate appointments across two weeks. Here it's three conversations across twenty minutes.

02

You talk to the actual people

Not a call centre, not a lead form. The owners, estimators and installers work these booths themselves — so you get straight answers about timelines, materials and what a job really costs.

03

See it before you commit

Run your hand over the countertop. Open and close the window. Stand in the shower surround. Photos and websites hide a lot; a display floor doesn't.

04

Straight answers on cost

Ask what a job like yours actually runs, and how long it takes, without sitting through an in-home sales appointment to find out.

05

Local businesses, local accountability

Many exhibitors are Green River BIA members working within a short drive of your house. If something goes sideways in year three, they're still here.

06

It costs you nothing

Admission is free thanks to our presenting sponsors. Parking is free. Bring the family, walk the floor, leave with a folder of ideas and no obligation.

Is this for me?

Come if you're any of these

You don't need a budget, a blueprint or a plan. Plenty of people walk in just to see what's possible.

Planning a project this year

You know what you want done and you need quotes, timelines and someone reliable to do it. Bring measurements and photos — several exhibitors will ballpark a number on the spot.

Thinking about building

Meet custom builders, developers and lenders in the same afternoon. Ask about lot availability, current per-square-foot ranges and how long a build is actually taking right now.

Just bought a house

New homeowners get the most out of the show. Line up an HVAC service plan, a lawn guy, a pest contract and a gutter quote before the first season turns.

Fixing one specific thing

A leaking roof, a garage door that won't close, windows that fog. Walk the aisles, find the three companies that handle it, and let them compete for the job.

Working on the outside

Decks, patios, pools, retaining walls, irrigation and planting. Spring is the busy season — booking in March is the difference between a June install and an October one.

Here for the ideas

No project, no pressure. Displays, product demos, community exhibits and the Master Gardeners. Come for an hour, leave with a Pinterest board's worth of real-world options.

Make it count

How to work the floor

A little prep turns a nice wander into a genuinely productive afternoon.

  • Photograph the problem before you come. A picture of the actual roof, room or yard gets you a far more useful answer than a description.
  • Bring rough measurements. Window openings, room dimensions, linear feet of gutter — enough for a ballpark.
  • Come Saturday morning if you want time. Exhibitors are freshest and the aisles are quietest in the first two hours.
  • Ask about lead times, not just cost. Spring fills up fast — who can actually start in June is often the more useful answer.
  • Bring a bag. You will leave with more brochures than you expect.
  • Walk the whole floor before you commit. The company you needed is often in the last aisle.
